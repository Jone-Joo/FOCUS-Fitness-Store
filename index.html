Training day guide

Build your gym session around the right machine.

Pick the target for the day, start with a controlled warmup, then use machines that keep the movement clean and easy to repeat.

01 Warm Up 8-12 minutes
02 Main Machine 3-4 sets
03 Accessories 2-3 movements
04 Cool Down stretch and breathe

Cardio warmup

Treadmill

Start with an easy pace and gradually increase your speed to get your heart rate up safely.

  • Great for a 10-minute warmup before lifting.
  • Walk or jog at a comfortable speed.
  • Keep your body upright and drop your arms naturally.

Push day

Chest Press

Adjust the seat so the handles align with your chest, then push forward smoothly.

  • Works your chest, shoulders, and triceps.
  • Keep your back flat against the pad.
  • Push controlled and avoid locking your elbows completely.

Pull day

Lat Pulldown

Hold the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width and pull it down toward your upper chest.

  • Builds a strong back and targets your biceps.
  • Pull with your elbows, not just your hands.

Leg day

Leg Press

Place your feet hip-width apart on the platform, lower it slowly, and push back up.

  • Targets your thighs, glutes, and hamstrings.
  • Keep your knees moving straight in line with your feet.
  • Never lock your knees at the top of the movement.
