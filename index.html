Training day guide

Build your gym session around the right machine.

Pick the target for the day, start with a controlled warmup, then use machines that keep the movement clean and easy to repeat.

01 Warm Up 8-12 minutes
02 Main Machine 3-4 sets
03 Accessories 2-3 movements
04 Cool Down stretch and breathe

Cardio warmup

Treadmill

Start easy, keep your chest tall, and increase speed gradually until breathing feels active but controlled.

  • Use it before any workout day.
  • Walk or jog for 8-12 minutes.
  • Keep hands relaxed instead of leaning on the rails.

Push day

Chest Press

Set the seat so the handles line up with the middle of your chest, then press forward with steady control.

  • Targets chest, shoulders, and triceps.
  • Keep shoulder blades back against the pad.
  • Stop before locking the elbows hard.

Pull day

Lat Pulldown

Grip slightly wider than your shoulders, pull the bar toward your upper chest, then return slowly.

  • Targets lats, upper back, and biceps.
  • Drive elbows down, not backward only.
  • Avoid swinging your torso to move the weight.

Leg day

Leg Press

Place feet hip-width on the platform, lower with control, and push through the middle of your foot.

  • Targets quads, glutes, and hamstrings.
  • Keep knees tracking in the same line as toes.
  • Do not lock the knees at the top.
